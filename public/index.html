Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a record?

One record is counted each time the workflow action successfully writes a value to a contact or company. If a run fails, for example because the record no longer exists or HubSpot returns an error, it is not counted.

When does my monthly usage reset?

Usage resets automatically on the 1st of every calendar month (UTC), whatever plan you are on.

What happens when I reach my limit?

The action stops processing new records for the rest of the month and the workflow history shows a "limit reached" error. We never charge overage fees. Upgrade at any time to continue immediately.

How do I upgrade, downgrade or cancel?

Open the app's settings page in HubSpot. From there you can buy a plan, or open the billing portal to change plan, update your card, download invoices or cancel. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, then your portal returns to the Free plan.

Do you store my CRM data?

No. Activity records are read from HubSpot only while the action runs and are held in memory briefly (up to a few minutes) so repeated runs are faster. We store only what is needed to run your account: your portal ID, access tokens, plan and monthly usage count. See our Privacy Policy.

Which HubSpot plans does it work with?

Any HubSpot account that can use custom workflow actions in Workflows, which is typically Professional or Enterprise tiers of Sales, Marketing, Service or Operations Hub.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you are not satisfied, request a refund within 14 days of a charge and we will refund it in full. Details are in our Refund Policy.
