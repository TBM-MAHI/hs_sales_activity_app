2. The App

The App is a HubSpot Marketplace app that adds a custom workflow action to your HubSpot account. When a contact or company is enrolled in the action, the App reads the activities associated with that record (calls, emails, meetings, notes and tasks) and writes one of the following results to a property you select:

  • most recent activity type;
  • most frequent activity type;
  • last meeting date;
  • last call date; or
  • last call outcome.

At installation the App creates an Activity Tracking property group and five text properties on contacts and companies. Results reflect the activity data available in HubSpot at the moment the action runs; they are not updated automatically unless the action runs again.
