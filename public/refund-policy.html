Refund Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not happy with a paid plan of the Contact-company Activity Tracking Tool, ask for a refund within 14 days of a charge and you will get a full refund. No questions asked.

1. Who processes refunds

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles refunds and returns. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method by Paddle.

2. Eligibility

  • New subscriptions: you can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment.
  • Renewals: you can request a full refund of a monthly renewal charge within 14 days of that charge, for example if you forgot to cancel.
  • Upgrades: the additional amount charged for an upgrade can be refunded within 14 days of the upgrade charge.
  • After 14 days, charges are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • The Free plan is free of charge, so there is nothing to refund.

We may decline refund requests that show clear abuse, for example repeated purchase-and-refund cycles. This does not affect any rights you have under applicable consumer law.

3. How to request a refund

Use either option:

  • Email support@twinkleflow.com with your HubSpot portal ID and the email used at checkout or the order number from your Paddle receipt.
  • Contact Paddle directly through paddle.net or the link in your receipt email.

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Once approved, refunds usually appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

4. What happens after a refund

When a subscription charge is refunded, the related subscription is cancelled and your HubSpot portal returns to the Free plan (50 records per month). The App stays installed and properties already written to your records are not changed.

5. Cancelling without a refund

You can cancel at any time from the billing portal linked in the App's settings page in HubSpot. Cancelling stops future renewals; your paid plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. Uninstalling the App from HubSpot does not cancel your subscription by itself.

6. Contact

Questions about refunds or billing: support@twinkleflow.com. See also our Terms & Conditions.
